### Full Description (Formatted for Google Play Console)

```text
NutriLog is a minimalist, privacy-first nutrition and macro tracker designed for intuitive daily logging, smart nutritional intelligence, and effortless cross-device synchronization.

Whether your goal is muscle gain, fat loss, athletic performance, or mindful eating, NutriLog provides instant clarity on your daily calories and macronutrients without the clutter, subscriptions, or aggressive ads found in typical trackers.

KEY FEATURES:

🎯 INTUITIVE DAILY DIARY & LIVE MACRO RINGS
• Real-time visual progress rings for Calories, Protein, Carbohydrates, and Healthy Fats.
• Categorized meal logging for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks.
• Quick hydration tracker (+250ml / +500ml one-tap water logging).
• Sleek Material Design 3 interface with dark mode and dynamic theming.

📸 AI MEAL & INGREDIENT PHOTO SCANNER
• Snap a picture of your plate, meal prep container, or recipe ingredients.
• Powered by advanced multimodal AI to analyze ingredients and estimate serving portions, calories, and complete macronutrient breakdowns.
• One-tap import directly into your selected meal category.

🏷️ NUTRITION FACTS LABEL SCANNER
• Point your camera at any packaged food's FDA-style Nutrition Facts label.
• Automatically extracts calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, dietary fiber, sodium, and serving sizes.
• Dynamic portion recalculation: slide or enter the exact grams you ate to scale macros precisely.

🔍 BARCODE SCANNER & FOOD SEARCH
• Instant barcode scanner powered by Open Food Facts with millions of global products.
• Comprehensive food search with offline database support and repeat meal memory for fast logging.

📊 HISTORICAL ANALYTICS & TREND CHARTS
• Interactive 7-day, 14-day, and 30-day analytics dashboards.
• Calorie intake trend charts with daily target threshold line and on-target compliance percentages.
• Stacked macronutrient distribution charts to visualize nutritional balance over time.
• Hydration averages and consistency metrics.

☁️ GOOGLE DRIVE WI-FI AUTO-SYNC
• Seamless cross-device backup: automatically queues your food entries and syncs to your personal Google Drive when connected to Wi-Fi.
• Zero cellular data usage: protects your mobile data plan by holding uploads until unmetered Wi-Fi joins.
• Easily export and restore your entire diary across Android phones and tablets.

🛡️ 100% PRIVATE & LOCAL-FIRST
• All nutrition records, meals, and custom targets remain in your device's private SQLite database.
• Zero third-party ad brokers and zero data monetization.
• No mandatory accounts or logins required to use core tracking features.

PERMISSIONS DISCLOSURE:
• Camera: Required exclusively for barcode scanning and taking photos of meals or nutrition labels.
• Internet: Required for AI food analysis, barcode product lookup, and Google Drive synchronization.
• Access Network State: Used to detect unmetered Wi-Fi connections for data-saving auto-sync.

DEVELOPER CONTACT & SUPPORT:
• Developer: Mike Jaron
• Email: MikeJaron@gmail.com
• Privacy Policy: Accessible directly within the in-app Settings menu or at your published Privacy Policy link.
```
